On **19–20 October 2026**, members of the **KLASTER+** team – **Tadeusz Daszczyński, Łukasz Rokicki, Marcin Szewczyk, Szymon Stoczko and Robert Wójtowicz** – attended meetings in Gdańsk with representatives of **[“Siarkopol” Gdańsk S.A.](https://siarkopol.gda.pl/)**, **[EnH2O Sp. z o.o.](https://enh2o.com.pl/)** and the **[Gdańsk Branch of the Institute of Power Engineering](https://www.ien.gda.pl/)**.

### Meeting Participants

**Siarkopol Gdańsk S.A.** was represented by **Jakub Papierski** – CEO, **Rafał Cichosz** – Head of the Process Utilities Department, and **Sabina Sadowska-Orzeszko** – Head of HR.

**EnH2O Sp. z o.o.** was represented by **Mariusz Krużyński** – CEO, **Krzysztof Rozmysłowski** – Head of Energy Affairs, and **Henryk Denkiewicz** – Head of the Power Grid Department. **Bartłomiej Zosiuk**, CEO of **AT PROJECT Sp. z o.o.**, also attended the meeting.

The visit was initiated by **Tadeusz Daszczyński**, KLASTER+ Project Manager.

## Meeting at Siarkopol and EnH2O – 19 October

### Presentations of Siarkopol, EnH2O and the KLASTER+ Project

The first meeting was hosted by **“Siarkopol” Gdańsk S.A.**, a member of the **PERN Group**. Participants were welcomed by **Jakub Papierski**, CEO, who presented the company's operations and its role within the **PERN Group**. Siarkopol provides cargo handling, storage and logistics services at the Port of Gdańsk.

**Mariusz Krużyński**, CEO, presented the activities of **EnH2O Sp. z o.o.** The company operates in areas associated with the Gdańsk Shipyard, where it also serves as an **OSDn distribution system operator**.

![Meeting of representatives of KLASTER+, Siarkopol, EnH2O and IEN-PIB](/assets/posts/2026-10-19-klaster-plus-gdansk-visit/siarkopol_00.webp)
*The KLASTER+ team meets representatives of Siarkopol, EnH2O and IEN-PIB at Siarkopol Gdańsk.*

In the next part of the meeting, **Bartłomiej Zosiuk**, CEO of **AT PROJECT Sp. z o.o.**, presented a concept developed for Siarkopol and EnH2O.

**Tadeusz Daszczyński**, KLASTER+ Project Manager, then presented the project's concept, objectives and planned activities. The project focuses on developing the functions of a **field controller operating at the interface between a microgrid and a distribution system operator's network**. The controller is intended to enable information exchange with the DSO and coordinate the operation of local energy sources, energy storage systems and loads in line with grid operating conditions.

### Visits to Technical Facilities of Siarkopol and EnH2O

After the presentations, participants visited the **Siarkopol Gdańsk** cargo terminal. **Rafał Cichosz**, Head of the Process Utilities Department, introduced selected elements of the company's infrastructure, including **transformer substations** and a **water treatment facility**.

**Mariusz Krużyński**, CEO of EnH2O, and **Henryk Denkiewicz**, Head of the Power Grid Department, then presented EnH2O's electrical power infrastructure.

## Visit to the Gdańsk Branch of the Institute of Power Engineering – 20 October

### IEN-PIB Research Activities, Technologies and Products

The meeting at the **Gdańsk Branch of the Institute of Power Engineering – National Research Institute** was led by **Michał Izdebski**, Branch Director. He presented the branch's history, selected research achievements, technologies and products.

![Meeting at the Gdańsk Branch of IEN-PIB](/assets/posts/2026-10-19-klaster-plus-gdansk-visit/ien.webp)
*The KLASTER+ team meets Michał Izdebski, Director of the Gdańsk Branch of IEN-PIB.*

One of the achievements presented was the development and implementation of the **GreenAVR** excitation and voltage regulation system for a **1000 MVA** generator at the South Ukraine Nuclear Power Plant. The use of **RTDS-HIL** technology in the system's development and testing was also discussed.

The discussions also covered energy transition challenges, including **grid-forming** technology, the anti-blackout package and **dynamic line rating (DLR)**. Other topics included wind condition estimation, **load-frequency control (LFC)** and real-time simulations.

### RTDS-HIL Laboratory

Participants also visited the **RTDS-HIL laboratory**, where the RTDS simulator is used to simulate electrical power systems in real time.

![RTDS-HIL laboratory at the Gdańsk Branch of IEN-PIB](/assets/posts/2026-10-19-klaster-plus-gdansk-visit/ien_rtds.webp)
*Michał Izdebski introduces the KLASTER+ team to the RTDS-HIL laboratory at the Gdańsk Branch of IEN-PIB.*

In a **hardware-in-the-loop (HIL)** configuration, simulation models interact with physical automation, protection and control devices.

The laboratory visit was particularly relevant to KLASTER+, as the project includes **verification of a physical field controller interacting with a microgrid model in an HIL environment**.
