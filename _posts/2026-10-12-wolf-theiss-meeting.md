On 12 October 2026, KLASTER+ team members Michał Połecki, Łukasz Rokicki and Tadeusz Daszczyński met with representatives of the law firm Wolf Theiss: Paweł Łączkowski, Partner and Head of Energy & Natural Resources in Poland, and Jakub Steciuk, Associate.

![Meeting at the Wolf Theiss office](/assets/posts/2026-10-12-wolf-theiss-meeting/2026-10-12-wolf-theiss-meeting.webp)
*Meeting at the Wolf Theiss office. From left to right: Jakub Steciuk,<br>Paweł Łączkowski, Tadeusz Daszczyński, Michał Połecki, Łukasz Rokicki.*

Wolf Theiss is an international law firm operating across Central, Eastern and Southeastern Europe (CEE/SEE), with more than 800 staff, including over 500 lawyers. The firm provides comprehensive legal advice to businesses in areas including mergers and acquisitions (M&A), banking and finance, capital markets, real estate, employment, competition, tax and dispute resolution.

Achieving the objectives of the KLASTER+ project requires collaboration beyond academia. Building lasting relationships with business partners is therefore an important part of our approach.

The meeting focused on opportunities for cooperation, particularly on bringing together the experience and expertise of both sides and strengthening connections with the business community. The participants also discussed joint initiatives that could open the door to new partnerships and projects.

We would like to thank Paweł Łączkowski and Jakub Steciuk for an engaging discussion. We look forward to the opportunity to work together.
