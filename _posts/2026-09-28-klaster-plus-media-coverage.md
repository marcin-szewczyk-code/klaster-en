An article on **KLASTER+** has been published on the **Warsaw University of Technology** website. It presents the project and the field controller being developed to support interaction between a microgrid and the DSO network.

> **KLASTER+ controller – microgrid operation and interaction with the DSO network**
>
> - Article on the WUT website: [read the article](https://www.pw.edu.pl/aktualnosci/sterownik-klaster-czyli-autonomiczne-zarzadzanie-mikrosiecia-i-wspolpraca-z-siecia-osd) [in Polish, accessed: 29-09-2026]
> - Article copy: [download PDF](/assets/posts/2026-09-28-klaster-plus-media-coverage/2026-09-28-klaster-plus-media-coverage.pdf) (in Polish)
> - Media information: [Media section](/media/)

The article outlines the main project objectives, the technical concept of autonomous microgrid management, and the role of cooperation between research and industry in the project.

It also presents the broader context of KLASTER+, including distributed energy systems, renewable energy integration, resilience of local energy systems, public acceptance of the energy transition, and energy poverty.
