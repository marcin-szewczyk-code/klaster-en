**Łukasz Rokicki** and **Michał Połecki**, members of the **KLASTER+** team from the Division of Power Grids and Systems at Warsaw University of Technology, delivered a training session on **DIgSILENT PowerFactory** for other members of the **KLASTER+** team and representatives of **Elektrometal Energetyka S.A.**

The session strengthened the team’s technical skills and provided an opportunity to explore the capabilities of **DIgSILENT PowerFactory**. This advanced software is used to model and analyze power systems and plays an important role in the design and integration of renewable energy sources.
