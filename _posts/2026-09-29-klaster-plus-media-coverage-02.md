An article on **KLASTER+** has been published on the **Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Warsaw University of Technology** website. It presents the project, its team, and the field controller being developed within the project. Information about the project was also published on the Faculty of Electrical Engineering WUT LinkedIn profile.

> **Faculty of Electrical Engineering researchers involved in the KLASTER+ project**
>
>- Article on the Faculty of Electrical Engineering WUT website: [read the article](https://www.ee.pw.edu.pl/blog/2026/09/29/pracownicy-wydzialu-elektrycznego-w-projekcie-klaster/) [in Polish, accessed: 30-10-2026]
>- Article copy: [download PDF](/assets/posts/2026-09-29-klaster-plus-media-coverage-02/klaster-plus-media-coverage.pdf) (in Polish)
>- Media information: [Media section](/media/)

The article presents the project team and the main technical concept of the KLASTER+ solution. It describes the functionality of the field controller located at the point of connection between the microgrid and the DSO network, including data exchange with management systems, maintaining power flows within the required limits, increasing self-consumption of locally generated energy, and reducing the need to disconnect renewable energy sources.

The article also highlights the cooperation between research institutions, microgrid operators, and industry.
