On 29 September 2026, a meeting on local energy and energy security was held in Lesznowola as part of the “Green Lesznowola” programme. The programme is part of the project “Improving the Energy Efficiency of Educational Buildings in the Lesznowola Municipality”, funded under Poland’s National Recovery Plan (***pl. KPO***).

**Dr Tadeusz Daszczyński**, KLASTER+ Project Leader, took part in the panel discussion **“Local Energy as a Foundation for Security and Development.”**

The panel also included:

- Andrzej Olbrysz – Lesznowola Municipality,
- Łukasz Madej – ESA HUB Ltd.

The discussion was moderated by Piotr Olewiński from ESA HUB.

The panel covered local energy security, energy costs, renewable energy sources, energy poverty, and the role of local energy for businesses.

The meeting also included a presentation on the local energy model, the role of microgrids, and the planned energy community, followed by a Q&A session with residents.
